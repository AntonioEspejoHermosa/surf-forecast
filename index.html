About this forecast

Beach surf is a preliminary breaking-height estimate. The profiles show the shape of offshore energy across six integrated 10–30 s period bands, with the strongest available band scaled to the estimated beach Hs. All profiles share one height axis. This display scaling is not a calculation of the nearshore spectrum. Missing bands remain gaps; partial and zero spectra are identified. Offshore Hs is shown separately. Quality follows beach-relative wind conditions. Wind colours use fixed thresholds in knots, including when speeds are displayed in m/s. Wave energy is an offshore per-wave proxy in kJ/m. Weather icons summarize cloud and rain. Water temperature is a sea-surface skin proxy. TPXO9 tide heights are model-relative, use no chart datum and exclude storm surge. Tides use a fixed −2 to +2 m axis; event labels show the actual supplied heights.
