About this forecast

Beach surf is a preliminary breaking-height estimate; bars use a fixed 0–5 m scale. Quality follows beach-relative wind conditions. Period remains an input to the wave model. Spectrum icons show relative energy in the six available 10–30 s period bands, integrated over all directions. Each icon uses its own scale: its height describes the shape, not the total swell energy. The intervals have different widths; this is energy per band, not continuous spectral density. Missing bands are gaps; a dash means no spectral data. Band heights and energy values are available in the forecast details. Wave energy is an offshore per-wave proxy in kJ/m. Weather summarizes cloud and rain; intense rain does not confirm lightning. Water temperature is a sea-surface skin proxy. TPXO9 tide levels are model-relative, exclude storm surge, and use no chart datum.
