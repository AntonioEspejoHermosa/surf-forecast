Sunrise uses the standard solar horizon; dusk is the end of civil twilight. Shading follows these solar transitions, independently of forecast steps. Compasses show the primary swell (teal), wind sea (coral) and secondary swell (dashed). Circle diameter is proportional to offshore Hs on a shared scale; radial position shows mean period and bearing shows the direction FROM. Arrows show wave travel. The shore line uses the forecast beach orientation. Each compass samples the first available forecast record in a six-hour interval; other rows retain every forecast time. Missing components remain unavailable. Component values require a compatible ECMWF source; the public feed may not include these fields. Wave and tide shading share midnight-to-midnight day boundaries. Beach Hs is an estimate. Water temperature is a surface-skin proxy. Tides are model-relative, exclude storm surge and use a fixed −2 to +2 m axis.
