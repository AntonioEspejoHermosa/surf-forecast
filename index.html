Sunrise uses the standard solar horizon; dusk is the end of civil twilight. Shading follows these solar transitions, independently of forecast steps. Spectra show offshore Hs per original integrated period band on a shared scale. The smooth curves are visual guides through those band heights; missing bands remain gaps. The view ends at 22 s. Beach Hs is an estimate. Water temperature is a surface-skin proxy. Tides are model-relative, exclude storm surge and use a fixed −2 to +2 m axis.
